**Kachi AI, Inc.** works on answer engine optimization (AEO) — making sure LLMs,
agentic browsers, and AI agents can read your website, understand it, and cite it.
For B2B and B2C marketing teams.

We don't just track what the models say. We change what they read.

## Two products

### Kachi — on-domain AEO

We fix your marketing site so AI can use it.

| Step | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Access** | Make your site legible to the crawlers and agents that feed the models. |
| **Comprehension** | Score every page for how well an LLM can understand it. |
| **Influence** | Map content gaps against the real questions buyers ask. |

### Kachi Brand Intelligence

Measure how AI answer engines **discover, describe, prefer and trust** your brand —
across the buyer prompts that matter, with sample size, confidence and evidence
behind every score. Nothing is scored until a human approves the rubric, prompts
and competitor set.
